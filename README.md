# **Supermarket Sales Analysis**

## **Project Overview**

This project analyzes supermarket sales data using python, pandas, and matplotlib. The analysis focuses on sales performance, product performance, customer behavior, payment methods, and branch-wise revenue.

## **Objective**

The objective of this project is to analyze supermarket sales and identify important business trends and insights that can help management improve sales performance and customer satisfaction.

## **Tools & Technologies**

-Python
-Pandas
-Matplotlib
-Jupyter Notebook
-CSV

## **Analysis Performed**

-Data loading and exploration
-Data cleaning 
-Missing value analysis
-Duplicate record checking
-Total sales calculation 
-Branch-wise sales analysis
-Product-wise sales analysis
-Payment method analysis
-Customer type analysis
-City-wise sales analysis
-Customer rating analysis

## **Visualizations**

The project includes:

-Branch-wise Sales
-Product-wise Sales
-Payment Method Distribution
-City-wise Sales
-Customer Type Distribution
-Customer Rating Distribution
-Sales by Payment Method

## **Key Business Insights**

-Branch B generated the highest total sales.
-Electronics was one of the strongest revenue-generating product categories.
-Cash and Card were commonly used payment methods.
-Customer ratings were generally positive.
-Sales performance varied across branches and cities.

## **Recommendations**

-Increase inventory for high-performing products.
-Improve marketing strategies for lower-performing branches.
-Introduced loyalty offers to encourage repeat purchases.
-Continue supporting multiple payment methods.
-Focus promotional campaigns on high-performing product categories.

## **Files**

## **Conclusion**

The analysis provides an overview of supermarket sales performance and identifies important patterns across branches, products, customers, and payment methods. These insights can support better inventory management, marketing decisions, and customer engagement strategies.
